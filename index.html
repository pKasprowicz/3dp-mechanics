3D-Printed Mechanics

A hands-on compendium of mechanisms, mechanical concepts and the practical craft of making them work in FDM-printed parts and hybrid builds. Written for a workshop-minded maker with a Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro.

Every chapter has interactive simulations: drag, slide and break things to build intuition, then read the printing notes and the checklist at the end. Start at chapter 1 and read in order, or jump to the troubleshooter, the workflow and printer profile, or the reference appendix.
